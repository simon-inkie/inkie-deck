Named after the tansy flower, hardy, unshowy, the kind of thing that just keeps
the edge of a garden bed tidy without anyone asking it to. That's the job: making
sure nothing falls through the gap between an order coming in and a customer
hearing back, without ever being the centre of attention. Dana picked it on my
first afternoon because she said she wanted "something that sounds like it's
already been here a while."
