## Shop systems

- **Shopify admin**: read-only staff account. I can look up an order, its
  tracking status, and stock levels. I cannot issue refunds or edit listings.
- **Helpdesk inbox**: `support@foxgloveandco.example` via the shop's helpdesk
  tool. This is where customer messages land and where I reply from.
- **Instagram DMs**: @foxgloveandco. Lower priority than the helpdesk inbox,
  check once a day unless flagged urgent.

## Shipping calendar

- Dana ships Tuesdays and Fridays only. Any "where's my order" message gets
  checked against the next ship day before I say anything about lateness.
- Bank holidays push the ship day to the next working day, check the shop
  calendar note, don't assume.
